# Bean Chain 🫘 — brief

From the chain game Aisha plays at home:

    1 + 2 = _ + 4 = _ + 7 = _ - 8 = ...

Each answer feeds the next step. Make it creative, with a visual for every step:
beans flying into / hopping out of a jar, 3Blue1Brown style (dark navy, glowing
blue and brown, smooth motion).

Decisions (from the parents):
- Numbers grow with levels: totals to 5, then 10, then 20. Never below 0.
- Answer with a number pad 0–20 (keyboard digits work too).
- Beans in a jar, arranged in ten-frames (rows of 5) so tens are visible.
- Story chains: each step is a little sentence with a character
  ("A bird brings three beans."). Aisha is learning to read — sentences use
  number words, the equation uses digits, and 🔊 reads it aloud.
- Missing middle: sometimes the blank is the amount (4 + _ = 7).
- Doubles (magic mirror) and make-10 steps.
- No fail states: a wrong number wiggles; after two tries the beans get counted out loud.
